## Sorting the way people read

A plain alphabetical sort compares characters by their code, which surprises people in three ways: capital letters sort before lowercase ones, accented letters end up after Z, and numbers inside text sort digit by digit so that version 10 lands before version 2. This tool fixes all three by default. Ignore case keeps apple and Apple together, Language-aware uses your browser's collation for your language, and Natural order reads numbers as numbers.

Every sort is stable, so lines that compare as equal keep their original order. That property lets you sort by more than one key: sort by the secondary key first, press the arrow to feed the result back in, then sort by the primary key.

## Columns, lengths and shuffles

For delimited data, By column sorts whole rows by one field, such as a date in the third column of a CSV export. Shortest first is handy for finding stray fragments in a list of names or keywords. Shuffle produces a random order that stays the same until you press Reshuffle, which is useful for drawing winners or randomising a quiz.
