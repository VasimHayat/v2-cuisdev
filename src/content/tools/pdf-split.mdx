## Split a PDF without uploading it

Splitting is usually about sending only what someone needs: the signed pages of a contract, one chapter of a manual, a single month from a year of statements. This tool opens the PDF in your browser, shows every page as a thumbnail, and writes the parts you choose as new PDFs on your own device.

The split options cover the common cases. Every N pages breaks a long scan into equal batches. Custom ranges handle anything irregular, one file per range. One file per page is handy for invoices that were scanned together. Odd and even pages separate the fronts and backs of a double-sided scan.

## What stays the same

Each page is copied into its new file rather than rendered as an image, so the output is identical to the original: the same fonts, the same vector drawings, searchable text and the original page size. You can still rotate pages that were scanned sideways, delete blank pages or move pages around before splitting, and the result list on the right updates as you go.
