## Tip, total and everyone's share

Splitting a restaurant bill is simple arithmetic that is easy to get wrong after dinner. This calculator takes the bill, the tip rate and the number of people and gives you four numbers: the tip, the total, the tip per person and what each person pays. The comparison table underneath shows the same bill at other common rates, so you can see what moving from 15% to 20% actually costs.

## The formulas

- Tip base = bill, or bill − tax when tipping on the pre-tax amount
- Tip = tip base × rate ÷ 100
- Total = bill + tip
- Each share = total ÷ people

With a tax percent, the tax inside a bill is bill − bill ÷ (1 + rate ÷ 100), so a 110 bill with 10% tax contains 10 of tax and 100 before tax. Rounding up adds to the tip rather than the bill, which is how most people round in practice.
