## Changing image formats

Image formats are a compromise between size, quality and compatibility. PNG is exact but large, JPG is small but has no transparency, and WebP and AVIF are newer formats that beat both on size. A converter is how you move an image to the format the next system wants: a CMS that only takes JPG, a design file that needs PNG, or a website that should serve WebP.

This converter handles whole batches. Add a folder of screenshots or photos, choose the target format, and every image is redrawn and encoded in your browser. Names keep their stem and get the right extension, and duplicates are numbered inside the zip.

## Lossless and lossy targets

Converting to PNG keeps every pixel exactly as decoded, which is right for text, diagrams and anything you will edit again. JPG, WebP and AVIF are lossy: the quality setting trades detail for size. For photos, WebP at quality 85 is a reliable default. When transparency matters, choose PNG, WebP or AVIF, because JPG fills transparent areas with a solid colour.
