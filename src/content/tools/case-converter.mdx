## Why convert case

Text changes case more often than you would think. A heading pasted in capitals needs to become Title Case, a sentence written with Caps Lock on needs fixing, and a column name from a spreadsheet needs to become a variable name that follows your codebase's convention. Doing it by hand is slow and error-prone, especially for long lists.

This converter shows every case side by side as you type. Pick the one you need, copy it, and move on. The programmer cases share one word splitter that understands existing camelCase, acronyms and digits, so converting between conventions is lossless: `lastUpdatedAt`, `last_updated_at` and `Last-Updated-At` all describe the same three words.

## Prose cases and identifier cases

Prose cases keep your punctuation and spacing and only change letters. That includes UPPER, lower, Title, Sentence, aLtErNaTiNg and InVeRsE, the last being the quickest fix for text typed with Caps Lock stuck on. Identifier cases throw punctuation away and join the words with a convention: no separator for camelCase and PascalCase, underscores for snake_case, hyphens for kebab-case and Train-Case, dots for dot.case and slashes for path/case.
